# Asset provenance and reuse notes

The shield sprite is the original `dist/assets/kalima-shields.png` used in the Kalima application. Rank names and thresholds match its existing `dist/data/manifest.json` on 2026-11-06. The source code in this package is standalone demo glue created for reuse.

Audio in `assets/` and the accompanying preview video derives from game recordings and audio clips supplied by the project's user. Such files may contain third-party copyrighted audio from Battlefield; redistribution, commercial use, or incorporation into a public app may require rights from the relevant owner. Replace third-party audio with licensed alternatives before shipping a public application.

The files are not automatically wired into the existing Kalima production app; they are a separate reusable example.
